Our Sketchpad remake is faithful to Ivan Sutherland's original design. The user interface conventions we take for granted today did not exist back then. Instead of a desktop mouse with integrated buttons, you would hold a light pen up to the display with one hand and press toggle switches with the other. Sketchpad is a two-handed interface.

There are only two shapes that you can draw with Sketchpad. The first is straight lines. Here's Timothy Johnson demonstrating.
