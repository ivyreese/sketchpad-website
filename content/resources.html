Sutherland's Thesis: SketchPad, A Man-Machine Graphical Communication System (1963)

The most authoritative copy is hosted by MIT, but there's a version hosted by Cambridge that might be easier to read.

1963 Technical Report

This technical report is based on the thesis. You can find it hosted by Bret Victor.

Technology and Courage (1982-1996)

Sutherland's view on how to pursue goals, including info on a "warm up project" from 1961 that preceded Sketchpad. You can find it hosted by Bret Victor.

Sketchpad by Ivan Sutherland at Bay Area Computer History Perspectives (1994)

Ivan's own telling of the Sketchpad system, hosted by YouTube.
