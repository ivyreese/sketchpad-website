Ivan Sutherland's Sketchpad
The Origin of Computer Graphics
---

A striking number of foundational ideas in computing first appeared at the MIT Lincoln Laboratory in 1963, on a computer the size of a warehouse, invented by a lone PhD student working 3–6 a.m.

This website honours the indelible influence of this great work.
